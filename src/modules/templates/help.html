Key Features
🗄️ Interactive Drawer
A sleek, stowable roster interface. Fully event-driven for real-time status updates without the lag.
🧭 Universal Compass & Focus
Track players in maps or normal rooms! Hovering cards, chat names, or characters reveals a spinning 3D indicator and halo glow.
🔔 Chat Highlighting
Highlights messages containing your name or custom words. Includes color matching, auto-capitalization, and exclusion phrases to prevent false positives.
🛡️ Privacy Mode
A customizable "boss key" that instantly blanks the character screen or game window. Toggle via keybind (Default: CTRL + ALT + B).
⚡ Smart Performance
Monitors true frame delta. Dynamically lowers visual quality and update rates to save your FPS during lag spikes.
👁️ Immersive Blindness
Optional roster blurring based on your blindness level. Fully respects BCX "Full Blind" rules.
✨ Smart Colors
Automatically detects dark or muddy player name colors and applies a high-contrast outline for readability.
💬 Whisper+
Send whispers in map rooms at any distance. Auto-elevates to a beep if a friend leaves the room before you send.
Core Commands
/roster [argument]
Opens the enhanced roster in the chat log. Shows detailed information about everyone in the room.
Arguments:
• count - Only shows current room population counters.
• admins - Only lists room administrators.
• vips - Only lists room whitelisted users.
• banner - Force redraws the room information banner.
• help - Shows this documentation.
/whisper+ [number] [msg] or /w+
Global whisper system for map rooms. Bypass range restrictions to message anyone on the map.
/dropkeys [color/all]
Instantly drop specific keys (bronze, silver, gold) or all keys at once.
Interactions & Navigation
  • Click Badge: Opens the character's focus screen.
  • Click Player's Name: Start a Whisper+ message.
  • Click Number (ID): Copies the player's member number to your clipboard.
  • Live Sorting: Use the top-right dropdown in the drawer to sort players by Roles, D/s Family, Lovers, or Friends.
  • Reverse Hover: Hovering a character in the game world highlights their card in the roster.
  • Keyboard Shortcuts: CTRL + ALT + D toggles the drawer. CTRL + ALT + B toggles Privacy Mode.

Config & Sync
  • Cloud Sync: Push your CRABS settings to the server to share across devices, or keep them strictly local.
  • Export/Import: Easily copy your settings to the clipboard to back them up or share them.
  • Restore Defaults: Reset all settings to their original state (includes a confirmation prompt).
  • Update Notifications: Toggle alerts that let you know when to refresh for a new version.
Drawer
  • Disable Drawer UI: Turns off the sliding drawer entirely. The roster will only print directly in the chat log.
  • /roster toggles drawer: Typing /roster or /crabs opens the drawer instead of printing to the chat window.
  • Hide Drawer Tab: Removes the persistent visual tab on the side of the screen.
  • Static Logo: Disables the animated logo in the drawer to save performance.
  • Auto-stow Options: Automatically close the drawer after sending a chat or Whisper+ message.
Chat & Highlights
  • Name/Word Highlights: Get alerted when specific words are spoken. Customize the highlight color.
  • Exclusion Phrases: Set specific phrases (e.g., "pick* a rose") to prevent false positive highlights.
  • Auto-Capitalize: Automatically capitalizes your name when detected in chat.
Immersion & Maps
  • Hardcore Lock & Respect BCX: Prevents UI cheating by enforcing blindness, gags, and BCX rules.
  • Compass & Focus Follows Mouse: Automatically switch to a player's page when clicking their card. Enable the 3D spinning indicator in standard rooms.
  • SuperZoom: Unlocks the game's default map zoom limitations (auto-disables if conflicts are detected).
