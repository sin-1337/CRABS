Core Commands
/roster [argument]
Opens the enhanced roster. Shows detailed information about everyone in the room.
Arguments:
• count - Only shows current room population counters.
• admins - Only lists room administrators.
• vips - Only lists room whitelisted users.
• banner - Force redraws the room information banner.
• help - Shows this documentation.
/whisper+ [number] [message] or /w+
Global whisper system for map rooms. Bypass range restrictions to message anyone on the map.
/dropkeys [color/all]
Instantly drop specific keys (bronze, silver, gold) or all keys at once.
Key Features
Interactive Side Drawer
A sleek, stowable interface providing quick access to the Roster and Help. It auto-hides when focusing on characters or typing chat (configurable in settings).
Map Compass
When hovering over a player's ID in the roster, a directional arrow appears on the map pointing toward their location.
Immersive Blindness & BCX Integration
Optional blurring of the roster based on your character's blindness level. Fully respects BCX "Full Blind" rules when enabled.

Interactions
  • Click Badge: Opens the character's focus/interaction screen.
  • Click Name/ID: Prepares a Whisper+ command for that player.
  • Right-Click Name/ID: Copies the player's member number to your clipboard.
  • Hover ID: Activates the Map Compass to track their position.
